Privacy policy

How Beninlife collects and uses your personal data. Last updated 16 October 2026.

The short version. We keep your email, a scrambled copy of your password, your username and what you do in the game, so the game works. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads. Voice chat goes straight between players and is never recorded. You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to delete it, at any time.

Voice chat

Voice chat is off until you switch it on. When it is on, your voice travels directly between your device and the devices of players standing near you in the game (this is called peer-to-peer). We do not record or store it. Because it is direct, those players' devices learn your IP address, and a Google service (STUN) is used only to help the two devices find each other. Blocked players can never hear you.

Who else handles your data

We use these service providers ("data processors") to run Beninlife. They may only use your data to provide their service to us:

  • Render runs the game server (USA)
  • Neon hosts the database (USA)
  • Vercel serves the game's web pages (USA and worldwide)
  • Resend sends password-reset emails (USA)
  • Sentry collects error reports (USA)

Because these providers are outside Nigeria, your data is transferred abroad. We rely on the safeguards the NDPA allows for this (sections 41–43), including the providers' contractual data protection terms. You can ask us for details.

Other players see your username, your character, what you say near them, and anything you send them. We may disclose data if the law requires it, for example to the police with a valid legal request.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and game data: while your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days.
  • Password-reset links: they expire after 30 minutes and are used once.
  • Reports and bans: up to 2 years after they are closed, so repeated abuse can be recognised.
  • Server logs and error reports: up to 90 days.
  • Database backups roll over and are gone within a few days.

Your rights

Under the NDPA (sections 34–38) you have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used (this page)
  • get a copy of your data, including in a format you can take elsewhere
  • have wrong data corrected
  • have your data deleted
  • object to how we use it, or have that use restricted
  • withdraw any consent you gave, without affecting what happened before
  • not be subject to decisions made only by automated means

To use any of these, email REPLACE-WITH-YOUR-EMAIL from the address on your account. We'll answer within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

Connections to Beninlife are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed with scrypt. We limit how many times anyone can guess a password. A password reset signs out every device. If a breach of personal data happens that is likely to put you at risk, we will tell the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours and tell you as soon as we can, as the NDPA requires.

Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser keeps a sign-in token in local storage so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it.

Changes

Beninlife is in beta and this policy may change. If we change it in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the game before the change applies.
